Discover Abuja

Website Planning Document

1. Site Name

Discover Abuja, Nigeria

Discover Abuja is a website about interesting places, activities, food, and culture in Abuja, Nigeria. I chose this name because it is simple and clearly describes what the website is about.

2. Site Purpose

The website will give information about different places to visit and things to do in Abuja. It will include places such as Millennium Park, Jabi Lake, Aso Rock, the National Christian Centre, the National Assembly, and Transcorp Hilton Abuja. It will also include information about Nigerian foods and some parts of Abuja's culture. Visitors will be able to view pictures, learn about different places, save their favorite places, and use a contact or feedback form.

3. Scenarios

  • What are some interesting places I can visit in Abuja?
  • What activities can I enjoy when visiting Abuja?
  • What Nigerian foods and cultural experiences can I discover in Abuja?

4. Color Schema

The website will use simple colors inspired by Nigeria and the natural environment of Abuja.

Dark Green - #0B5D1E

Used for the header, navigation, headings, and important sections.

Gold - #D4A017

Used for buttons, accents, borders, and highlights.

White - #FFFFFF

Used for the main background and content areas.

Dark Gray - #333333

Used for normal paragraph text.

5. Typography

Poppins

Poppins will be used for the website title, headings, and navigation.

Roboto, sans-serif

Roboto or sans-serif will be used for paragraphs, descriptions, forms, and other general body text.
